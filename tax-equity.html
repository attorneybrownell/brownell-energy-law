Capability

Tax Equity & Safe Harbor

The documentation discipline that keeps investment tax credits intact — from beginning-of-construction through transfer or partnership flip.

Tax credits are won or lost on paper. Beginning-of-construction qualification under the IRS safe harbor framework, foreign-entity-of-concern sourcing rules, prevailing wage and apprenticeship records, domestic content substantiation — each is a documentation regime where the difference between a defensible file and a fatal gap is knowing exactly what an auditor or tax equity counsel will ask for, years before they ask.

This practice builds those files as a system: qualification memos backed by a documentation index, organized the way tax equity diligence actually proceeds. The kit format means your CFO gets a board-ready summary and your project team gets a runbook of exactly which documents to collect and retain.

Typical matters

  • 5% safe harbor and physical work test qualification analysis
  • Beginning-of-construction documentation packages for audit and diligence
  • FEOC / prohibited foreign entity compliance reviews of procurement
  • Tax equity term sheet review and partnership document analysis
  • Credit transfer transaction support
