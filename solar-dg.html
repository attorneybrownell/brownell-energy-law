C&I Solar & Distributed Generation

The home ground of this practice: the contracts, financings, and compliance programs that carry a commercial-scale solar project from term sheet to operation.

Commercial and industrial solar lives in a gap. Projects between half a megawatt and twenty megawatts are too complex for form documents and too small to absorb big-firm fees — yet each one runs on the same stack of high-stakes agreements as a utility-scale plant: a power purchase agreement, site control, an interconnection agreement, an EPC contract, and a financing structure that has to survive tax-equity and lender diligence.

Four decades of general-counsel work in this exact space means the issues surface fast: the assignment clause that will block your financing, the early-termination formula that quietly shifts value to the offtaker, the site-control gap that stalls a portfolio sale. And the Full Stack format means the answer arrives in a form your whole team can use — memo, deck, visualization, audio brief, and runbook, in two business days.

Typical matters

  • Power purchase agreements — drafting, review, and negotiation, buyer or seller side
  • Community solar subscription structures and state program compliance
  • Rooftop, carport, and ground-mount site leases, easements, and licenses
  • Portfolio and single-project acquisitions — diligence through closing
  • Membership interest purchase agreements and development-stage transfers
  • State incentive program enrollment and compliance (NY, NJ, MD, ME, MA, and beyond)
