Interconnection & Grid

The agreements and utility processes that connect a project to the grid — and the traps buried inside them.

Interconnection is where project schedules go to die. Cost allocations arrive as non-negotiable exhibits, milestone deadlines carry forfeiture consequences, and a seemingly minor design change can trigger a material-modification review that sends a project to the back of the queue. The legal work is less about redlining — utilities rarely negotiate — and more about knowing exactly which obligations bite, when, and what your options are.

A Counsel Kit for an interconnection agreement translates the document into an operating plan: what you owe, what you must do by when, what design freedom you retain, and where the agreement's real risks sit. Your engineering and development teams get a milestone tracker, not a memo they'll never open.

Typical matters

  • Interconnection agreement review — distribution and small-generator
  • Cost-allocation, security, and upgrade-obligation analysis
  • Material modification risk assessments before design changes
  • Queue position, deadline, and extension strategy
  • Utility coordination and dispute correspondence
