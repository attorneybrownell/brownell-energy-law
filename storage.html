Capability

Battery Storage

Standalone and paired storage projects, from offtake structure through warranty, augmentation, and safety compliance.

Storage contracts look like power contracts until they don't. Revenue stacks span capacity, energy, and ancillary services; performance guarantees hinge on degradation curves and augmentation obligations rather than simple availability; and the warranty package — cycling limits, state-of-charge windows, thermal event carve-outs — determines whether the asset's economics survive year ten.

This practice brings a power-sector GC's transactional toolkit to those documents: the same financeability discipline applied to solar PPAs, tuned to the failure modes that are unique to batteries. Every engagement ships as a Full Stack deliverable — the analysis, a board deck, a one-page risk picture, an audio brief, and a runbook your operations team can execute.

Typical matters

  • Storage tolling, capacity, and hybrid offtake agreements
  • Battery supply agreements — warranty, degradation, and augmentation terms
  • Solar-plus-storage structuring and shared-facilities issues
  • Safety, permitting, and fire-code compliance programs
  • O&M and long-term service agreements for storage assets
