Services & Pricing

Published prices. Defined scope. Two-day delivery.

Every service ships as the Full Stack — instrument, board deck, visualization, audio brief, and runbook — and comes in two tiers. First Cut is the complete deliverable, shipped within two business days of payment or your money back. To Signature adds the negotiation: I carry the matter through an executed agreement, with three negotiation rounds included and additional rounds at $950 each. Elect it when you order, or after you've seen the First Cut. Prices are standard fees for the scope described; unusually complex matters get an adjusted fixed price in the SOW before you pay, never after.

How To Signature works

Hourly billing makes the meter a mystery. Rounds make it a map.

A negotiation round is one exchange: your counterparty responds, I analyze their markup, we align on strategy in a call, and I return the next draft with supporting correspondence. Three rounds close most C&I deals. If a counterparty digs in past three, additional rounds are $950 each — published, countable, and always your call to continue. You can elect To Signature when you order, or after the First Cut arrives; either way you know the shape of the whole engagement before a single round begins.

The delivery guarantee

On time, or every dollar back.

Payment is due at order — and in exchange, the delivery date is contractual. If a First Cut misses its ship date, the full fee is refunded. No dispute process, no negotiation.
