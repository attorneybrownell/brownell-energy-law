Why this practice exists

Outside counsel is priced for companies you haven't become yet.

A mid-size firm bills $700–$1,100 an hour and answers in weeks. A full-time general counsel costs $300k+ before benefits. If you're developing, acquiring, or operating power projects at small-company scale, both options tax every decision you make. This practice is built on a different premise: you should pay for judgment, not for the hours it takes to type the deliverable. A modern AI production toolchain manufactures the work product; four decades of energy-GC experience directs it, verifies it, and signs it.

The signature deliverable

Every engagement ships as a Counsel Kit — five formats, one price.

A law firm sends you a memo and considers the job done. A Counsel Kit arms your whole company: the CEO walks into the board meeting with slides, the CFO plays the audio brief on the drive to the lender call, and your ops team works from a checklist instead of a footnote.

How it works

From request to signed statement of work in minutes — not weeks.

Every request clears a conflicts check before any engagement issues. Usually same day.

Pay the fixed fee when you accept the SOW — card or ACH. Work starts when payment clears. No invoices aging, no surprises accruing at six-minute increments.
