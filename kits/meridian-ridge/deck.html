3 / 8
Blocker 1 · § 11.2

Buyer can walk away for the price of the steel.

  • Termination for convenience on 180 days' notice, paying only unamortized capital cost
  • Payment ignores lost margin, tax-credit recapture, and financing breakage
  • No tax equity investor closes against a revocable revenue stream
  • Ask: delete the right — or price it with a real termination-value schedule
4 / 8
Blockers 2 & 3 · §§ 14.1, 16

Buyer holds a veto over our financing — and offers no credit for theirs.

  • Assignment clause requires Buyer consent, sole discretion, even for collateral assignment to lenders
  • Most routinely conceded fix in the market: financing carve-outs + consent & estoppel
  • Counterparty is an unrated shell; 25 years of revenue needs a parent guaranty or letter of credit

Negotiation strategy

Lead with financeability. Trade the cheap gives for the caps.

  • Round 1: Issues 1–3 as one package — "what any financeable PPA requires," not a commercial ask
  • Round 2: hold the performance-guarantee cap; give appraisal fix and mutual indemnity cheaply
  • Round 3: concede insurance limits last, against the credit support we need
  • Walk-away line: no deal without the financing carve-outs and real credit support
7 / 8
Recommendation

Authorize the counterproposal. Target execution in six weeks.

  • Approve the redline and the ask/fallback matrix as presented
  • Counsel to deliver counterproposal to Granite Peak this week
  • Report back after Round 1 with movement map
  • With the package accepted: sign, then start interconnection and financing workstreams in parallel
