How your team uses this

  • Before each call: the deal lead reviews the open rows for that round — asks and fallbacks are pre-authorized, so no mid-call improvisation.
  • During: anything Buyer offers between "ask" and "fallback" can be accepted on the spot; anything below fallback gets parked, not rejected.
  • After: counsel updates status and circulates the one-line delta. The board sees movement, not memos.
