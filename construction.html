EPC & Construction

Owner-side construction contracting for power projects — built for the realities of distributed generation budgets and schedules.

An EPC contract is a risk-allocation machine, and at C&I scale the owner usually operates it without a construction lawyer in the room. The terms that matter — liquidated damages that actually compensate for lost incentives, security that survives contractor distress, warranty obligations that outlast commissioning, lien-waiver discipline that keeps title clean for financing — are exactly the terms a form contract quietly waters down.

Forty years of owner-side construction contracting, delivered in kit form: the redline itself, a deck explaining the risk trades to your board, and a milestone-payment and lien-waiver runbook your project manager runs the job from.

Typical matters

  • EPC and balance-of-plant contract drafting and negotiation
  • Liquidated damages, performance guarantee, and security structuring
  • Change order, delay, and force majeure disputes
  • Subcontractor settlement and completion-risk strategy
  • Commissioning, substantial completion, and punch-list disputes
